The game

Come back before there is nothing left to come back to.

Years after childhood summers spent on his grandmother's farm, the protagonist returns to a place that now stands damaged and silent. The world outside is worse. Roads are empty. Towns are dangerous. The infected roam by day and grow more aggressive after dark.

The farm is not just a base. It is the heart of the game. Repair buildings, fences and the well, grow food, bring back power, unlock forgotten tools and slowly turn ruin into shelter.

“

The goal is not to save the world. It is to make one small place worth living in again.

01

Scavenge

Leave the farm to search abandoned places for food, tools, materials and rare equipment.

02

Survive

Fight with improvised melee weapons and scarce firearms while managing the risk of staying out too long.

03

Rebuild

Restore the old farm step by step. Every repair should make the place feel more alive and useful.

04

Learn by doing

Progress through practical experience, books, blueprints and discoveries instead of grinding XP for its own sake.
